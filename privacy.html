Holy Hunch privacy policy

Last updated 13 November 2026.

In short

Holy Hunch has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no purchases, and it does not sell or share your information. Most of what you do stays on your device. A few optional features send text or audio to our server so they can work; they are described below.

What stays on your device

Your game scores and progress, saved stories, settings, unfinished puzzles, your place in readings, and copies of narrated audio are stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them. Exporting your progress from Settings creates a file that you choose where to send.

Conversations with saints and guides

When you type a question to a saint or a historical guide, the question, the last few turns of that conversation and which guide you are talking to are sent to our server, which passes them to an AI provider (Groq) to write the reply. The replies are AI portrayals, not the real people. Please do not include personal details in your questions. Our server does not store your questions or the replies; the AI provider handles them under its own privacy policy (groq.com/privacy-policy).

Voice questions

If you choose to ask a question by voice, the app records only while you hold the microphone, then sends that recording to our server to be turned into text by the AI provider. The recording is deleted from your device once it has been sent, and our server does not keep it. The microphone is never used otherwise.

Natural voices

When a natural voice reads a story or a reply aloud, the text to be read is sent to our server and the AI provider to make the audio. The audio is kept on your device so it does not need to be made again. The device's own voice works without sending anything.

Reporting a reply

If you report an AI reply, we receive the reply's text, which guide gave it, the reason you chose and the time. Nothing that identifies you is included. We keep reports to review and improve the guides.

Playing with others

Nearby games use Bluetooth to connect phones in the same room; nothing leaves the room. Internet games pass game messages, including the name you type when you join, between the players through our server. The server keeps rooms only while they are open and keeps nothing afterwards.

Permissions

Bluetooth is used only to find and join nearby games. Motion is used only for optional tilt controls. The microphone is used only for voice questions you choose to record. The calendar is used only when you choose to save a reminder.

Our server

Like most internet services, the company that hosts our server may keep short-lived technical logs, such as the network address a request came from, to keep the service running and protect it from abuse. We do not use them to identify you.

Children

Holy Hunch is made for families and does not ask for anyone's name, email or other personal details to use it. The conversation features are AI portrayals and are best explored with a parent or teacher.
