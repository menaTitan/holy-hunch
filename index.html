Bible party games, saints trivia, sacred-art puzzles and a 3D walk through Bible and Church history, for families, Sunday schools and youth groups.

Common questions

Do I need an account? No. There are no accounts, ads or purchases.

Where is my progress kept? On your device. Deleting the app deletes it.

Are the saints' replies real? No. The talking icons are AI portrayals, labelled in the app, with sources to check. Any reply can be reported from the conversation.

How do I play with friends on their own phones? In Clue Board, one person hosts and the others join, in the same room over Bluetooth or anywhere over the internet with a game code.
